# Wellbeing — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Wellbeing does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## What is stored
Mood entries, check-ins, journal reflections, plans, habits, meditation progress, trusted contacts and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

## Who can see it
Only you. We have no servers and cannot access your entries. If you turn on app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by your device; we never receive biometric data.

## Notifications
Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service receives your data.

## Sharing
Data leaves your device only when you choose to — for example using Export Data, sending a message to a trusted contact, or calling a helpline.

## Deleting your data
Use Settings → Clear All Data, or delete the app. Both permanently remove everything stored by Wellbeing on your device.

## Children
Wellbeing is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and with the app update.
