# Wellbeing: Mood & Calm – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

This policy explains what the Wellbeing app ("Wellbeing", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Wellbeing is provided by Prathap Adicherla, who is the data controller. Contact: [wellbeingsupport247@gmail.com](mailto:wellbeingsupport247@gmail.com).

## Summary

- Your moods, check-ins, journal, plans, habits, therapy notes and contacts are stored **only on your device**.
- The AI coach is optional. If you use it, a limited summary is sent to generate a reply, and we do not store it.
- An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address, an optional first name and a securely hashed password.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your device

Mood entries, daily check-ins, journal reflections, mental state plans, habits and goals, meditation progress, therapy companion notes, trusted contacts and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. We have no access to them. If you turn on app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by your device; we never receive biometric data.

## AI coach (optional)

The coach only works after you agree to it in the app. When you use it, we send the following to our server and to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model, to generate a reply:

- your answers to the coach's short quiz and your optional first name;
- your mood ratings from the last two weeks (for example "good" or "bad", with how many days ago) and your latest sleep, energy and stress scores;
- the question you choose, plus up to three earlier questions and replies from the same day's conversation.

Your notes, journal entries, therapy companion notes and contacts are **never** sent. Our server does not store or log questions or replies. Anthropic processes this data to provide the service under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited period for safety and misuse monitoring.

To enforce the daily question limit, the app creates a random ID for your install. It is not linked to your identity and is held only in the server's memory.

## Account (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address, optional first name, a securely hashed password (we never store or see your actual password) and the date you joined. We use this only to sign you in and to keep Wellbeing Plus on your devices.

If you reset your password, we email a one-time code to your address. The code is stored in hashed form and expires after 15 minutes. Emails are sent through Google (Gmail).

You can delete your account at any time in **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account details from our database.

## Subscriptions (Wellbeing Plus)

Wellbeing Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID if you are signed in) and your App Store purchase history for this app.

## Who processes data for us

- **Anthropic** – generates AI coach replies.
- **Replit** – hosts our server; account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database provided through Replit (Neon).
- **RevenueCat** – checks subscription status.
- **Google** – sends password reset emails.
- **Apple** – App Store purchases and app distribution.

These providers may process data in the United States. Where UK or EU data protection law applies, transfers rely on the providers' data protection terms, including Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

## Legal bases (UK and EU users)

- **Explicit consent** for the AI coach, which may involve information about your wellbeing. You can stop using the coach at any time.
- **Contract** to provide your account and Wellbeing Plus.
- **Legitimate interests** to keep the service secure and prevent misuse, such as rate limits on sign-in attempts.

## How long we keep data

- Data on your device: until you delete it in **Settings → Clear All Data** or delete the app.
- Coach questions and replies: not stored by us.
- Account details: until you delete your account.
- Password reset codes: deleted when used, or after 15 minutes.
- Daily question counters: reset every day.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Email [wellbeingsupport247@gmail.com](mailto:wellbeingsupport247@gmail.com) and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## Age

Wellbeing is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18.

## Not a medical service

Wellbeing supports self-care. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or crisis support. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above, and highlight significant changes in the app.
